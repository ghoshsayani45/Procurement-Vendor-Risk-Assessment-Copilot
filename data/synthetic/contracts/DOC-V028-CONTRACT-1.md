# Professional Services Agreement

**Between** Indrapath Technologies Ltd, Tower B, Sector 62, Noida, Uttar Pradesh 201309, India (the "Customer") **and** Bytemarsh Software Labs Pvt Ltd, India (the "Supplier").

**Status:** Final draft for signature (vendor's proposed terms)  
**Effective Date:** 01-Dec-2026  
## 1. Services
The Supplier will provide Software development outsourcing services to the Customer as described in Schedule 1. The Customer's business owner for this agreement is the Engineering function.

## 2. Term and renewal
The initial term of this Agreement is three (3) years from the Effective Date. This Agreement does not renew automatically.

## 3. Fees and payment
Annual fees are INR 2,60,00,000 (INR 2.6 crore), invoiced quarterly in arrears and payable within 30 days of invoice. Fees are subject to an annual price adjustment of up to 10 per cent with effect from each anniversary of the Effective Date.

## 4. Service levels
The Supplier will meet a monthly service availability of at least 99.5% as set out in Schedule 2, with service credits for shortfalls.

## 5. Limitation of liability
Except for fraud, the Supplier's maximum liability is limited to an amount equal to 18 months' fees under this Agreement. The Customer's total liability is limited to the fees payable in the 12 months preceding the claim.

## 6. Termination
Either party may terminate this Agreement without cause by giving not less than ninety (90) days' written notice.

## 7. Audit
On reasonable notice, the Supplier shall permit the Customer (or a third-party auditor) to inspect records and facilities relevant to the Services.

## 8. Confidentiality
Each party will keep the other's confidential information confidential and use it only for this Agreement.

## 9. Governing law
This Agreement is governed by the laws of India; the courts at New Delhi have exclusive jurisdiction.
