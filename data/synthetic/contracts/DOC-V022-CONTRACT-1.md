# SaaS Subscription Agreement

**Between** Indrapath Technologies Ltd, Tower B, Sector 62, Noida, Uttar Pradesh 201309, India (the "Customer") **and** Hollowpine Workspace Inc, United States (the "Supplier").

**Status:** Executed agreement  
**Effective Date:** 1 November 2023  
## 1. Services
The Supplier will provide Collaboration and email SaaS services to the Customer as described in Schedule 1. The Customer's business owner for this agreement is the Workplace Technology function.

## 2. Term and renewal
This Agreement starts on the Effective Date and continues for an initial term of 36 months. Thereafter it renews automatically for successive 12-month periods unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least 45 days before the end of the then-current term.

## 3. Fees and payment
Annual fees are INR 3,50,00,000 (INR 3.5 crore), invoiced quarterly in arrears and payable within 30 days of invoice. The Supplier may increase fees once per contract year by no more than 3%.

## 4. Service levels
The Supplier will meet a monthly service availability of at least 99.5% as set out in Schedule 2, with service credits for shortfalls.

## 5. Limitation of liability
The Supplier's total aggregate liability arising out of or in connection with this Agreement shall not exceed the fees paid or payable by the Customer in the 24 months preceding the event giving rise to the claim. The Customer's aggregate liability shall not exceed the total fees paid in the preceding twelve (12) months.

## 6. Termination
The Customer may terminate this Agreement for convenience on 90 days' written notice.

## 7. Audit
On reasonable notice, the Supplier shall permit the Customer (or a third-party auditor) to inspect records and facilities relevant to the Services.

## 8. Data protection and security incidents
The Supplier will process personal data only as the Customer's Data Processor and in accordance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. The parties' data processing agreement forms part of this Agreement. The Supplier shall notify the Customer of any personal data breach or security incident affecting Customer data within 36 hours of becoming aware of it. Customer personal data will be stored and processed in United States. Any transfer outside India is subject to the cross-border transfer safeguards in Schedule 3. The Supplier will not appoint a new sub-processor without the Customer's prior written approval.

## 9. Confidentiality
Each party will keep the other's confidential information confidential and use it only for this Agreement.

## 10. Governing law
This Agreement is governed by the laws of India; the courts at New Delhi have exclusive jurisdiction.
