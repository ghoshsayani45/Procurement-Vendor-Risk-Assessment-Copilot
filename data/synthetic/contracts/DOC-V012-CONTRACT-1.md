# Managed Services Agreement

**Between** Indrapath Technologies Ltd, Tower B, Sector 62, Noida, Uttar Pradesh 201309, India (the "Customer") **and** Tessaline IT Services Pvt Ltd, India (the "Supplier").

**Status:** Executed agreement  
**Effective Date:** 1 May 2024  
## 1. Services
The Supplier will provide IT staffing and managed services services to the Customer as described in Schedule 1. The Customer's business owner for this agreement is the IT Service Delivery function.

## 2. Term and renewal
This Agreement starts on the Effective Date and continues for an initial term of 36 months. This Agreement does not renew automatically.

## 3. Fees and payment
Annual fees are INR 40,00,000 (INR 40 lakh), invoiced quarterly in arrears and payable within 30 days of invoice. Fees are fixed for the initial term.

## 4. Service levels
The Supplier will meet a monthly service availability of at least 99.5% as set out in Schedule 2, with service credits for shortfalls.

## 5. Limitation of liability
Except for fraud, the Supplier's maximum liability is limited to an amount equal to 12 months' fees under this Agreement. The Customer's aggregate liability shall not exceed the total fees paid in the preceding twelve (12) months.

## 6. Termination
The Customer may terminate this Agreement for convenience on 90 days' written notice.

## 7. Audit
On reasonable notice, the Supplier shall permit the Customer (or a third-party auditor) to inspect records and facilities relevant to the Services.

## 8. Data protection and security incidents
Security incidents affecting Customer Data must be reported to the Customer without undue delay and in any event within 24 hours.

## 9. Confidentiality
Each party will keep the other's confidential information confidential and use it only for this Agreement.

## 10. Governing law
This Agreement is governed by the laws of India; the courts at New Delhi have exclusive jurisdiction.
