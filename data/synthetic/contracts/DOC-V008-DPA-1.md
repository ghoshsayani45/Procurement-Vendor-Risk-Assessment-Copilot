# Data Processing Agreement

**Data Fiduciary:** Indrapath Technologies Ltd  
**Data Processor:** Orbelle CRM Inc  
**Date of signature:** October 23, 2026  
**Main agreement:** SaaS Subscription Agreement  
1. **Subject matter.** Processing of personal data of the Customer's employees, customers and contractors needed to provide the services.
2. **Instructions.** The Processor processes personal data only on the documented instructions of the Fiduciary, as required by section 8(2) of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.
3. **Security.** The Processor maintains reasonable security safeguards, including encryption, access control and logging, consistent with Rule 6 of the DPDP Rules, 2025.
4. **Breach notification.** The Processor notifies the Fiduciary of any personal data breach within 48 hours of becoming aware of it.
5. **Location.** Personal data is stored and processed in United States. Transfers outside India rely on the safeguards in Annex 2.
6. **Sub-processors.** New sub-processors require the Fiduciary's prior written approval.
7. **Deletion.** On termination, personal data is deleted or returned within 30 days.
