# Professional Services Agreement

**Between** Indrapath Technologies Ltd, Tower B, Sector 62, Noida, Uttar Pradesh 201309, India (the "Customer") **and** Codeweft Technologies Pvt Ltd, India (the "Supplier").

**Status:** Executed agreement  
**Effective Date:** 1 October 2023  
## 1. Services
The Supplier will provide Software development outsourcing services to the Customer as described in Schedule 1. The Customer's business owner for this agreement is the Engineering function.

## 2. Term and renewal
This Agreement starts on the Effective Date and continues for an initial term of 36 months. On expiry, the term will automatically renew for further periods of one year each, unless a party notifies the other in writing not less than 90 days prior to the expiry of the current term.

## 3. Fees and payment
Annual fees are INR 1,50,00,000 (INR 1.5 crore), invoiced quarterly in arrears and payable within 30 days of invoice. Fees are fixed for the initial term.

## 4. Service levels
The Supplier will meet a monthly service availability of at least 99.5% as set out in Schedule 2, with service credits for shortfalls.

## 5. Limitation of liability
The Supplier's total aggregate liability arising out of or in connection with this Agreement shall not exceed the fees paid or payable by the Customer in the 18 months preceding the event giving rise to the claim. The Customer's aggregate liability shall not exceed the total fees paid in the preceding twelve (12) months.

## 6. Termination
Either party may terminate this Agreement without cause by giving not less than ninety (90) days' written notice.

## 7. Audit
The Supplier's annual independent assurance reports are the Customer's sole means of verifying compliance; the Customer has no right to audit the Supplier.

## 8. Confidentiality
Each party will keep the other's confidential information confidential and use it only for this Agreement.

## 9. Governing law
This Agreement is governed by the laws of India; the courts at New Delhi have exclusive jurisdiction.
