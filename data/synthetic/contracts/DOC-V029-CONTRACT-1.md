# SaaS Subscription Agreement

**Between** Indrapath Technologies Ltd, Tower B, Sector 62, Noida, Uttar Pradesh 201309, India (the "Customer") **and** Learnstead Academy Pvt Ltd, India (the "Supplier").

**Status:** Final draft for signature (vendor's proposed terms)  
**Effective Date:** December 1, 2026  
## 1. Services
The Supplier will provide E-learning SaaS services to the Customer as described in Schedule 1. The Customer's business owner for this agreement is the Learning & Development function.

## 2. Term and renewal
This Agreement starts on the Effective Date and continues for an initial term of 36 months. On expiry, the term will automatically renew for further periods of one year each, unless a party notifies the other in writing not less than 45 days prior to the expiry of the current term.

## 3. Fees and payment
Annual fees are INR 12,00,000 (INR 12 lakh), invoiced quarterly in arrears and payable within 30 days of invoice. The Supplier may increase fees once per contract year by no more than 7.5%.

## 4. Service levels
The Supplier will meet a monthly service availability of at least 99.5% as set out in Schedule 2, with service credits for shortfalls.

## 5. Limitation of liability
Except for fraud, the Supplier's maximum liability is limited to an amount equal to 18 months' fees under this Agreement. The Customer's total liability is limited to the fees payable in the 12 months preceding the claim.

## 6. Termination
The Customer may terminate this Agreement for convenience on 90 days' written notice.

## 7. Audit
The Customer or its appointed auditors may audit the Supplier's compliance with this Agreement once a year on 30 days' notice.

## 8. Data protection and security incidents
Security incidents affecting Customer Data must be reported to the Customer without undue delay and in any event within 24 hours.

## 9. Confidentiality
Each party will keep the other's confidential information confidential and use it only for this Agreement.

## 10. Governing law
This Agreement is governed by the laws of India; the courts at New Delhi have exclusive jurisdiction.
