# Managed Services Agreement

**Between** Indrapath Technologies Ltd, Tower B, Sector 62, Noida, Uttar Pradesh 201309, India (the "Customer") **and** Vaultaire Backup Pvt Ltd, India (the "Supplier").

**Status:** Executed agreement  
**Effective Date:** 2023-12-01  
## 1. Services
The Supplier will provide Backup and disaster recovery services to the Customer as described in Schedule 1. The Customer's business owner for this agreement is the IT Infrastructure function.

## 2. Term and renewal
The initial term of this Agreement is three (3) years from the Effective Date. Any renewal requires a written agreement signed by both parties.

## 3. Fees and payment
Annual fees are INR 1,30,00,000 (INR 1.3 crore), invoiced quarterly in arrears and payable within 30 days of invoice. The Supplier may increase fees once per contract year by no more than 3%.

## 4. Service levels
The Supplier will meet a monthly service availability of at least 99.5% as set out in Schedule 2, with service credits for shortfalls.

## 5. Limitation of liability
Except for fraud, the Supplier's maximum liability is limited to an amount equal to 12 months' fees under this Agreement. The Customer's aggregate liability shall not exceed the total fees paid in the preceding twelve (12) months.

## 6. Termination
The Customer may terminate this Agreement for convenience on 90 days' written notice.

## 7. Audit
The Customer or its appointed auditors may audit the Supplier's compliance with this Agreement once a year on 30 days' notice.

## 8. Data protection and security incidents
The Supplier will process personal data only as the Customer's Data Processor and in accordance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. The parties' data processing agreement forms part of this Agreement. Security incidents affecting Customer Data must be reported to the Customer without undue delay and in any event within 36 hours. Customer personal data will be stored and processed in India. The Customer may object to any new sub-processor within 15 days of notice; the Supplier will not use a sub-processor the Customer has objected to.

## 9. Confidentiality
Each party will keep the other's confidential information confidential and use it only for this Agreement.

## 10. Governing law
This Agreement is governed by the laws of India; the courts at New Delhi have exclusive jurisdiction.
