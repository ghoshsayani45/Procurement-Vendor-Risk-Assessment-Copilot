# SaaS Subscription Agreement

**Between** Indrapath Technologies Ltd, Tower B, Sector 62, Noida, Uttar Pradesh 201309, India (the "Customer") **and** Brightvale HR Systems Ltd, United Kingdom (the "Supplier").

**Status:** Final draft for signature (vendor's proposed terms)  
**Effective Date:** December 1, 2026  
## 1. Services
The Supplier will provide HRMS and payroll SaaS services to the Customer as described in Schedule 1. The Customer's business owner for this agreement is the HR Operations function.

## 2. Term and renewal
This Agreement starts on the Effective Date and continues for an initial term of 36 months. On expiry, the term will automatically renew for further periods of one year each, unless a party notifies the other in writing not less than 120 days prior to the expiry of the current term.

## 3. Fees and payment
Annual fees are INR 2,40,00,000 (INR 2.4 crore), invoiced quarterly in arrears and payable within 30 days of invoice. The Supplier may increase fees once per contract year by no more than 10%.

## 4. Service levels
The Supplier will meet a monthly service availability of at least 99.5% as set out in Schedule 2, with service credits for shortfalls.

## 5. Limitation of liability
The Supplier's total aggregate liability arising out of or in connection with this Agreement shall not exceed the fees paid or payable by the Customer in the 24 months preceding the event giving rise to the claim. The Customer's aggregate liability shall not exceed the total fees paid in the preceding twelve (12) months.

## 6. Termination
The Customer may terminate this Agreement for convenience on 90 days' written notice.

## 7. Audit
On reasonable notice, the Supplier shall permit the Customer (or a third-party auditor) to inspect records and facilities relevant to the Services.

## 8. Data protection and security incidents
The Supplier will process personal data only as the Customer's Data Processor and in accordance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. The parties' data processing agreement forms part of this Agreement. The Supplier shall notify the Customer of any personal data breach or security incident affecting Customer data within 72 hours of becoming aware of it. Customer personal data will be stored and processed in India. The Customer may object to any new sub-processor within 15 days of notice; the Supplier will not use a sub-processor the Customer has objected to.

## 9. Confidentiality
Each party will keep the other's confidential information confidential and use it only for this Agreement.

## 10. Governing law
This Agreement is governed by the laws of India; the courts at New Delhi have exclusive jurisdiction.
