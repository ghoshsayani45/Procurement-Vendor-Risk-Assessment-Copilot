# Managed Services Agreement

**Between** Indrapath Technologies Ltd, Tower B, Sector 62, Noida, Uttar Pradesh 201309, India (the "Customer") **and** Wardlock Security Operations LLC, United States (the "Supplier").

**Status:** Final draft for signature (vendor's proposed terms)  
**Effective Date:** 01-Dec-2026  
## 1. Services
The Supplier will provide Managed security (SOC) services services to the Customer as described in Schedule 1. The Customer's business owner for this agreement is the Information Security function.

## 2. Term and renewal
The initial term of this Agreement is three (3) years from the Effective Date. Thereafter it renews automatically for successive 12-month periods unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least 60 days before the end of the then-current term.

## 3. Fees and payment
Annual fees are INR 2,00,00,000 (INR 2 crore), invoiced quarterly in arrears and payable within 30 days of invoice. The Supplier may increase fees once per contract year by no more than 5%.

## 4. Service levels
The Supplier will meet a monthly service availability of at least 99.5% as set out in Schedule 2, with service credits for shortfalls.

## 5. Limitation of liability
The Supplier's total aggregate liability arising out of or in connection with this Agreement shall not exceed the fees paid or payable by the Customer in the 12 months preceding the event giving rise to the claim. Nothing in this Agreement limits the Customer's liability to the Supplier for any claim arising under it.

## 6. Termination
The Customer may terminate this Agreement for convenience on 90 days' written notice.

## 7. Audit
On reasonable notice, the Supplier shall permit the Customer (or a third-party auditor) to inspect records and facilities relevant to the Services.

## 8. Confidentiality
Each party will keep the other's confidential information confidential and use it only for this Agreement.

## 9. Governing law
This Agreement is governed by the laws of India; the courts at New Delhi have exclusive jurisdiction.
