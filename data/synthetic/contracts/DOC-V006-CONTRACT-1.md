# Managed Services Agreement

**Between** Indrapath Technologies Ltd, Tower B, Sector 62, Noida, Uttar Pradesh 201309, India (the "Customer") **and** Sentrivane Cyber Defence Pvt Ltd, India (the "Supplier").

**Status:** Executed agreement  
**Effective Date:** 1 July 2023  
## 1. Services
The Supplier will provide Managed security (SOC) services services to the Customer as described in Schedule 1. The Customer's business owner for this agreement is the Information Security function.

## 2. Term and renewal
The initial term of this Agreement is three (3) years from the Effective Date. Thereafter it renews automatically for successive 12-month periods unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least 90 days before the end of the then-current term.

## 3. Fees and payment
Annual fees are INR 1,20,00,000 (INR 1.2 crore), invoiced quarterly in arrears and payable within 30 days of invoice. Fees are subject to an annual price adjustment of up to 5 per cent with effect from each anniversary of the Effective Date.

## 4. Service levels
The Supplier will meet a monthly service availability of at least 99.5% as set out in Schedule 2, with service credits for shortfalls.

## 5. Limitation of liability
The Supplier's total aggregate liability arising out of or in connection with this Agreement shall not exceed the fees paid or payable by the Customer in the 12 months preceding the event giving rise to the claim. The Customer's aggregate liability shall not exceed the total fees paid in the preceding twelve (12) months.

## 6. Termination
The Customer may terminate only for the Supplier's uncured material breach or insolvency.

## 7. Audit
The Customer or its appointed auditors may audit the Supplier's compliance with this Agreement once a year on 30 days' notice.

## 8. Data protection and security incidents
The Supplier shall notify the Customer of any personal data breach or security incident affecting Customer data within 48 hours of becoming aware of it.

## 9. Confidentiality
Each party will keep the other's confidential information confidential and use it only for this Agreement.

## 10. Governing law
This Agreement is governed by the laws of India; the courts at New Delhi have exclusive jurisdiction.
