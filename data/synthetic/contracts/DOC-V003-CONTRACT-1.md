# SaaS Subscription Agreement

**Between** Indrapath Technologies Ltd, Tower B, Sector 62, Noida, Uttar Pradesh 201309, India (the "Customer") **and** Payvanta Workforce Solutions Pvt Ltd, India (the "Supplier").

**Status:** Executed agreement  
**Effective Date:** 1 September 2023  
## 1. Services
The Supplier will provide HRMS and payroll SaaS services to the Customer as described in Schedule 1. The Customer's business owner for this agreement is the HR Operations function.

## 2. Term and renewal
This Agreement starts on the Effective Date and continues for an initial term of 36 months. On expiry, the term will automatically renew for further periods of one year each, unless a party notifies the other in writing not less than 60 days prior to the expiry of the current term.

## 3. Fees and payment
Annual fees are INR 3,10,00,000 (INR 3.1 crore), invoiced quarterly in arrears and payable within 30 days of invoice. The Supplier may increase fees once per contract year by no more than 6%.

## 4. Service levels
The Supplier will meet a monthly service availability of at least 99.5% as set out in Schedule 2, with service credits for shortfalls.

## 5. Limitation of liability
Except for fraud, the Supplier's maximum liability is limited to an amount equal to 18 months' fees under this Agreement. The Customer's aggregate liability shall not exceed the total fees paid in the preceding twelve (12) months.

## 6. Termination
Either party may terminate this Agreement without cause by giving not less than ninety (90) days' written notice.

## 7. Audit
The Customer or its appointed auditors may audit the Supplier's compliance with this Agreement once a year on 30 days' notice.

## 8. Data protection and security incidents
The Supplier will process personal data only as the Customer's Data Processor and in accordance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. The parties' data processing agreement forms part of this Agreement. Customer personal data will be stored and processed in India. The Supplier will not appoint a new sub-processor without the Customer's prior written approval.

## 9. Confidentiality
Each party will keep the other's confidential information confidential and use it only for this Agreement.

## 10. Governing law
This Agreement is governed by the laws of India; the courts at New Delhi have exclusive jurisdiction.
