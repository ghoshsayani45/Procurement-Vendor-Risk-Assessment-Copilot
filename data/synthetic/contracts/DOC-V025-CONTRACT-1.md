# Telecom Services Agreement

**Between** Indrapath Technologies Ltd, Tower B, Sector 62, Noida, Uttar Pradesh 201309, India (the "Customer") **and** Meridux Telecom Pvt Ltd, India (the "Supplier").

**Status:** Executed agreement  
**Effective Date:** 01-Jun-2024  
## 1. Services
The Supplier will provide Telecom and connectivity services to the Customer as described in Schedule 1. The Customer's business owner for this agreement is the Network Services function.

## 2. Term and renewal
The initial term of this Agreement is three (3) years from the Effective Date. This Agreement does not renew automatically.

## 3. Fees and payment
Annual fees are INR 2,10,00,000 (INR 2.1 crore), invoiced quarterly in arrears and payable within 30 days of invoice. Fees are subject to an annual price adjustment of up to 6 per cent with effect from each anniversary of the Effective Date.

## 4. Service levels
The Supplier will meet a monthly service availability of at least 99.5% as set out in Schedule 2, with service credits for shortfalls.

## 5. Limitation of liability
The Supplier's total aggregate liability arising out of or in connection with this Agreement shall not exceed the fees paid or payable by the Customer in the 18 months preceding the event giving rise to the claim. The Customer's aggregate liability shall not exceed the total fees paid in the preceding twelve (12) months.

## 6. Termination
Either party may terminate this Agreement without cause by giving not less than ninety (90) days' written notice.

## 7. Audit
The Customer or its appointed auditors may audit the Supplier's compliance with this Agreement once a year on 30 days' notice.

## 8. Data protection and security incidents
Security incidents affecting Customer Data must be reported to the Customer without undue delay and in any event within 24 hours.

## 9. Confidentiality
Each party will keep the other's confidential information confidential and use it only for this Agreement.

## 10. Governing law
This Agreement is governed by the laws of India; the courts at New Delhi have exclusive jurisdiction.
