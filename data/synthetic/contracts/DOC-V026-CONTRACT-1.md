# Telecom Services Agreement

**Between** Indrapath Technologies Ltd, Tower B, Sector 62, Noida, Uttar Pradesh 201309, India (the "Customer") **and** Skyquill Connect Pvt Ltd, India (the "Supplier").

**Status:** Executed agreement  
**Effective Date:** 1 August 2023  
## 1. Services
The Supplier will provide Telecom and connectivity services to the Customer as described in Schedule 1. The Customer's business owner for this agreement is the Network Services function.

## 2. Term and renewal
The initial term of this Agreement is three (3) years from the Effective Date. Thereafter it renews automatically for successive 12-month periods unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least 120 days before the end of the then-current term.

## 3. Fees and payment
Annual fees are INR 60,00,000 (INR 60 lakh), invoiced quarterly in arrears and payable within 30 days of invoice. The Supplier may increase fees once per contract year by no more than 3%.

## 4. Service levels
The Supplier will meet a monthly service availability of at least 99.5% as set out in Schedule 2, with service credits for shortfalls.

## 5. Limitation of liability
Except for fraud, the Supplier's maximum liability is limited to an amount equal to 12 months' fees under this Agreement. The Customer's aggregate liability shall not exceed the total fees paid in the preceding twelve (12) months.

## 6. Termination
The Customer may terminate this Agreement for convenience on 90 days' written notice.

## 7. Audit
The Customer or its appointed auditors may audit the Supplier's compliance with this Agreement once a year on 30 days' notice.

## 8. Data protection and security incidents
Security incidents affecting Customer Data must be reported to the Customer without undue delay and in any event within 48 hours.

## 9. Confidentiality
Each party will keep the other's confidential information confidential and use it only for this Agreement.

## 10. Governing law
This Agreement is governed by the laws of India; the courts at New Delhi have exclusive jurisdiction.
