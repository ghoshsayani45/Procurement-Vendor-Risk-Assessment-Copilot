# Cloud Services Agreement

**Between** Indrapath Technologies Ltd, Tower B, Sector 62, Noida, Uttar Pradesh 201309, India (the "Customer") **and** Cirrolux Infrastructure Pte Ltd, Singapore (the "Supplier").

**Status:** Final draft for signature (vendor's proposed terms)  
**Effective Date:** 1 December 2026  
## 1. Services
The Supplier will provide Cloud hosting (IaaS) services to the Customer as described in Schedule 1. The Customer's business owner for this agreement is the IT Infrastructure function.

## 2. Term and renewal
This Agreement starts on the Effective Date and continues for an initial term of 36 months. This Agreement does not renew automatically.

## 3. Fees and payment
Annual fees are INR 7,20,00,000 (INR 7.2 crore), invoiced quarterly in arrears and payable within 30 days of invoice. Fees are subject to an annual price adjustment of up to 6 per cent with effect from each anniversary of the Effective Date.

## 4. Service levels
The Supplier will meet a monthly service availability of at least 99.5% as set out in Schedule 2, with service credits for shortfalls.

## 5. Limitation of liability
Except for fraud, the Supplier's maximum liability is limited to an amount equal to 6 months' fees under this Agreement. The Customer's aggregate liability shall not exceed the total fees paid in the preceding twelve (12) months.

## 6. Termination
Either party may terminate this Agreement without cause by giving not less than ninety (90) days' written notice.

## 7. Audit
No on-site or records audit by the Customer is permitted under this Agreement.

## 8. Data protection and security incidents
The Supplier will process personal data only as the Customer's Data Processor and in accordance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. The parties' data processing agreement forms part of this Agreement. The Supplier shall notify the Customer of any personal data breach or security incident affecting Customer data within 48 hours of becoming aware of it. Customer personal data will be stored and processed in Singapore. No additional safeguards apply to transfers. The Supplier may appoint or replace sub-processors without the Customer's consent.

## 9. Confidentiality
Each party will keep the other's confidential information confidential and use it only for this Agreement.

## 10. Governing law
This Agreement is governed by the laws of India; the courts at New Delhi have exclusive jurisdiction.
