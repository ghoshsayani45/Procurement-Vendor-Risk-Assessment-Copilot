# SaaS Subscription Agreement

**Between** Indrapath Technologies Ltd, Tower B, Sector 62, Noida, Uttar Pradesh 201309, India (the "Customer") **and** Orbelle CRM Inc, United States (the "Supplier").

**Status:** Final draft for signature (vendor's proposed terms)  
**Effective Date:** 01-Dec-2026  
## 1. Services
The Supplier will provide CRM SaaS services to the Customer as described in Schedule 1. The Customer's business owner for this agreement is the Sales Operations function.

## 2. Term and renewal
This Agreement starts on the Effective Date and continues for an initial term of 36 months. Any renewal requires a written agreement signed by both parties.

## 3. Fees and payment
Annual fees are INR 45,00,000 (INR 45 lakh), invoiced quarterly in arrears and payable within 30 days of invoice. Fees are subject to an annual price adjustment of up to 3 per cent with effect from each anniversary of the Effective Date.

## 4. Service levels
The Supplier will meet a monthly service availability of at least 99.5% as set out in Schedule 2, with service credits for shortfalls.

## 5. Limitation of liability
The Supplier's total aggregate liability arising out of or in connection with this Agreement shall not exceed the fees paid or payable by the Customer in the 12 months preceding the event giving rise to the claim. The Customer's aggregate liability shall not exceed the total fees paid in the preceding twelve (12) months.

## 6. Termination
The Customer may terminate this Agreement for convenience on 90 days' written notice.

## 7. Audit
The Customer or its appointed auditors may audit the Supplier's compliance with this Agreement once a year on 30 days' notice.

## 8. Data protection and security incidents
The Supplier will process personal data only as the Customer's Data Processor and in accordance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. The parties' data processing agreement forms part of this Agreement. Security incidents affecting Customer Data must be reported to the Customer without undue delay and in any event within 48 hours. Customer personal data will be stored and processed in United States. Any transfer outside India is subject to the cross-border transfer safeguards in Schedule 3. The Customer may object to any new sub-processor within 15 days of notice; the Supplier will not use a sub-processor the Customer has objected to.

## 9. Confidentiality
Each party will keep the other's confidential information confidential and use it only for this Agreement.

## 10. Governing law
This Agreement is governed by the laws of India; the courts at New Delhi have exclusive jurisdiction.
