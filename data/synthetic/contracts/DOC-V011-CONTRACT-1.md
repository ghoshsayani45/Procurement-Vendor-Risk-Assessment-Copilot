# Master Services Agreement

**Between** Indrapath Technologies Ltd, Tower B, Sector 62, Noida, Uttar Pradesh 201309, India (the "Customer") **and** Datafjord Analytics Pvt Ltd, India (the "Supplier").

**Status:** Executed agreement  
**Effective Date:** 1 May 2023  
## 1. Services
The Supplier will provide Data analytics and BI services to the Customer as described in Schedule 1. The Customer's business owner for this agreement is the Data & Analytics function.

## 2. Term and renewal
This Agreement starts on the Effective Date and continues for an initial term of 36 months. Thereafter it renews automatically for successive 12-month periods unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least 60 days before the end of the then-current term.

## 3. Fees and payment
Annual fees are INR 2,20,00,000 (INR 2.2 crore), invoiced quarterly in arrears and payable within 30 days of invoice. The Supplier may increase fees once per contract year by no more than 6%.

## 4. Service levels
The Supplier will meet a monthly service availability of at least 99.5% as set out in Schedule 2, with service credits for shortfalls.

## 5. Limitation of liability
Except for fraud, the Supplier's maximum liability is limited to an amount equal to 12 months' fees under this Agreement. The Customer's total liability is limited to the fees payable in the 12 months preceding the claim.

## 6. Termination
Either party may terminate this Agreement without cause by giving not less than ninety (90) days' written notice.

## 7. Audit
On reasonable notice, the Supplier shall permit the Customer (or a third-party auditor) to inspect records and facilities relevant to the Services.

## 8. Data protection and security incidents
The Supplier will process personal data only as the Customer's Data Processor and in accordance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. No separate data processing agreement has been executed. Security incidents affecting Customer Data must be reported to the Customer without undue delay and in any event within 48 hours. Customer personal data will be stored and processed in India. The Supplier will not appoint a new sub-processor without the Customer's prior written approval.

## 9. Confidentiality
Each party will keep the other's confidential information confidential and use it only for this Agreement.

## 10. Governing law
This Agreement is governed by the laws of India; the courts at New Delhi have exclusive jurisdiction.
