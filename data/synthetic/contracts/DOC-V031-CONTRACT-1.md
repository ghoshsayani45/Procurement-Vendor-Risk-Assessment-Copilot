# Business Process Outsourcing Agreement

**Between** Indrapath Technologies Ltd, Tower B, Sector 62, Noida, Uttar Pradesh 201309, India (the "Customer") **and** Voxenta Contact Centres Pvt Ltd, India (the "Supplier").

**Status:** Final draft for signature (vendor's proposed terms)  
**Effective Date:** 01-Dec-2026  
## 1. Services
The Supplier will provide Customer support outsourcing (BPO) services to the Customer as described in Schedule 1. The Customer's business owner for this agreement is the Customer Service function.

## 2. Term and renewal
The initial term of this Agreement is three (3) years from the Effective Date. Any renewal requires a written agreement signed by both parties.

## 3. Fees and payment
Annual fees are INR 5,50,00,000 (INR 5.5 crore), invoiced quarterly in arrears and payable within 30 days of invoice. The Supplier may increase fees once per contract year by no more than 6%.

## 4. Service levels
The Supplier will meet a monthly service availability of at least 99.5% as set out in Schedule 2, with service credits for shortfalls.

## 5. Limitation of liability
The Supplier's total aggregate liability arising out of or in connection with this Agreement shall not exceed the fees paid or payable by the Customer in the 18 months preceding the event giving rise to the claim. The limitations in this clause do not apply to the Customer, whose liability under this Agreement is unlimited.

## 6. Termination
Either party may terminate this Agreement without cause by giving not less than ninety (90) days' written notice.

## 7. Audit
The Customer or its appointed auditors may audit the Supplier's compliance with this Agreement once a year on 30 days' notice.

## 8. Data protection and security incidents
The Supplier will process personal data only as the Customer's Data Processor and in accordance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. No separate data processing agreement has been executed. The Supplier shall notify the Customer of any personal data breach or security incident affecting Customer data within 48 hours of becoming aware of it. Customer personal data will be stored and processed in India. The Supplier will not appoint a new sub-processor without the Customer's prior written approval.

## 9. Confidentiality
Each party will keep the other's confidential information confidential and use it only for this Agreement.

## 10. Governing law
This Agreement is governed by the laws of India; the courts at New Delhi have exclusive jurisdiction.
