# Professional Services Agreement

**Between** Indrapath Technologies Ltd, Tower B, Sector 62, Noida, Uttar Pradesh 201309, India (the "Customer") **and** Corvanta Talent Partners Pvt Ltd, India (the "Supplier").

**Status:** Final draft for signature (vendor's proposed terms)  
**Effective Date:** 2026-12-01  
## 1. Services
The Supplier will provide IT staffing and managed services services to the Customer as described in Schedule 1. The Customer's business owner for this agreement is the IT Service Delivery function.

## 2. Term and renewal
The initial term of this Agreement is three (3) years from the Effective Date. This Agreement does not renew automatically.

## 3. Fees and payment
Annual fees are INR 90,00,000 (INR 90 lakh), invoiced quarterly in arrears and payable within 30 days of invoice. The Supplier may increase fees once per contract year by no more than 5%.

## 4. Service levels
The Supplier will meet a monthly service availability of at least 99.5% as set out in Schedule 2, with service credits for shortfalls.

## 5. Limitation of liability
The Supplier's total aggregate liability arising out of or in connection with this Agreement shall not exceed the fees paid or payable by the Customer in the 12 months preceding the event giving rise to the claim. The Customer's total liability is limited to the fees payable in the 12 months preceding the claim.

## 6. Termination
The Customer may terminate only for the Supplier's uncured material breach or insolvency.

## 7. Audit
The Customer or its appointed auditors may audit the Supplier's compliance with this Agreement once a year on 30 days' notice.

## 8. Confidentiality
Each party will keep the other's confidential information confidential and use it only for this Agreement.

## 9. Governing law
This Agreement is governed by the laws of India; the courts at New Delhi have exclusive jurisdiction.
