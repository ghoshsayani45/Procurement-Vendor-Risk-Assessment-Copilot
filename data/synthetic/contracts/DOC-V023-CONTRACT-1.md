# Payment Services Agreement

**Between** Indrapath Technologies Ltd, Tower B, Sector 62, Noida, Uttar Pradesh 201309, India (the "Customer") **and** Paytrove Gateway Pvt Ltd, India (the "Supplier").

**Status:** Final draft for signature (vendor's proposed terms)  
**Effective Date:** 1 December 2026  
## 1. Services
The Supplier will provide Payment gateway services to the Customer as described in Schedule 1. The Customer's business owner for this agreement is the Treasury function.

## 2. Term and renewal
This Agreement starts on the Effective Date and continues for an initial term of 36 months. Any renewal requires a written agreement signed by both parties.

## 3. Fees and payment
Annual fees are INR 2,80,00,000 (INR 2.8 crore), invoiced quarterly in arrears and payable within 30 days of invoice. Fees are subject to an annual price adjustment of up to 10 per cent with effect from each anniversary of the Effective Date.

## 4. Service levels
The Supplier will meet a monthly service availability of at least 99.5% as set out in Schedule 2, with service credits for shortfalls.

## 5. Limitation of liability
Except for fraud, the Supplier's maximum liability is limited to an amount equal to 18 months' fees under this Agreement. The Customer's total liability is limited to the fees payable in the 12 months preceding the claim.

## 6. Termination
The Customer may terminate this Agreement for convenience on 90 days' written notice.

## 7. Audit
The Customer or its appointed auditors may audit the Supplier's compliance with this Agreement once a year on 30 days' notice.

## 8. Data protection and security incidents
The Supplier will process personal data only as the Customer's Data Processor and in accordance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. The parties' data processing agreement forms part of this Agreement. The Supplier shall notify the Customer of any personal data breach or security incident affecting Customer data within 96 hours of becoming aware of it. Customer personal data will be stored and processed in India. The Customer may object to any new sub-processor within 15 days of notice; the Supplier will not use a sub-processor the Customer has objected to.

## 9. Confidentiality
Each party will keep the other's confidential information confidential and use it only for this Agreement.

## 10. Governing law
This Agreement is governed by the laws of India; the courts at New Delhi have exclusive jurisdiction.
