# Supply and Managed Services Agreement

**Between** Indrapath Technologies Ltd, Tower B, Sector 62, Noida, Uttar Pradesh 201309, India (the "Customer") **and** Opticrest Surveillance Systems Pvt Ltd, India (the "Supplier").

**Status:** Final draft for signature (vendor's proposed terms)  
**Effective Date:** December 1, 2026  
## 1. Services
The Supplier will provide CCTV and facility IoT services to the Customer as described in Schedule 1. The Customer's business owner for this agreement is the Facilities Management function.

## 2. Term and renewal
This Agreement starts on the Effective Date and continues for an initial term of 36 months. Any renewal requires a written agreement signed by both parties.

## 3. Fees and payment
Annual fees are INR 1,00,00,000 (INR 1 crore), invoiced quarterly in arrears and payable within 30 days of invoice. The Supplier may increase fees once per contract year by no more than 7.5%.

## 4. Service levels
The Supplier will meet a monthly service availability of at least 99.5% as set out in Schedule 2, with service credits for shortfalls.

## 5. Limitation of liability
The Supplier's total aggregate liability arising out of or in connection with this Agreement shall not exceed the fees paid or payable by the Customer in the 12 months preceding the event giving rise to the claim. The Customer's aggregate liability shall not exceed the total fees paid in the preceding twelve (12) months.

## 6. Termination
This Agreement may be terminated only for material breach that remains unremedied 30 days after written notice. Neither party may terminate for convenience.

## 7. Audit
The Customer or its appointed auditors may audit the Supplier's compliance with this Agreement once a year on 30 days' notice.

## 8. Data protection and security incidents
The Supplier will process personal data only as the Customer's Data Processor and in accordance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. The parties' data processing agreement forms part of this Agreement. Security incidents affecting Customer Data must be reported to the Customer without undue delay and in any event within 48 hours. Customer personal data will be stored and processed in India. The Supplier will not appoint a new sub-processor without the Customer's prior written approval.

## 9. Confidentiality
Each party will keep the other's confidential information confidential and use it only for this Agreement.

## 10. Governing law
This Agreement is governed by the laws of India; the courts at New Delhi have exclusive jurisdiction.
