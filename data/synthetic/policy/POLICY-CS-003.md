# Contract Standards for Technology Vendors

**Indrapath Technologies Ltd · CS-003 · Version 2.0 · Effective 1 April 2026**

Every technology contract must meet these standards. Any deviation needs Legal Counsel approval and is recorded as a contract finding in the vendor risk assessment.

| # | Standard | Requirement |
|---|---|---|
| CS-1 | Vendor limitation of liability | Vendor liability cap of at least 12 months of fees paid or payable. Uncapped vendor liability is acceptable. |
| CS-2 | Customer liability | Indrapath Technologies Ltd's own liability to the vendor must be capped. |
| CS-3 | Auto-renewal | Auto-renewal is allowed only if the cancellation notice period is no more than 90 days before the end of the term. |
| CS-4 | Termination for convenience | Indrapath Technologies Ltd must be able to terminate for convenience on reasonable notice. |
| CS-5 | Audit rights | Indrapath Technologies Ltd (or its auditors) must have the right to audit the vendor's compliance with the contract. |
| CS-6 | Price escalation | Annual price increases of no more than 8.0%. |
| CS-7 | Amendments | An amendment changes the contract only from its effective date. Amendments signed but not yet effective do not change current obligations. |
| CS-8 | Governing law | Indian law and courts preferred; other governing law needs Legal Counsel approval. |
